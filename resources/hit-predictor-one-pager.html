How it works

The hard problem was never the model — it's the join. Connecting a title's creative attributes to its true commercial performance across fragmented ID systems is where every prior attempt died. HIT Predictor's 12.96M-row certified identity spine solves it — a certified 220K-title candidate pool, with title-integrity guards that caught and rejected a 25.6% bad-match rate that would silently poison anyone else's model.

01Resolve
Every title dual-anchored across metadata, industry ID registries, audience measurement, and social identity — Class-A certified crosswalks, not fuzzy guesses.
02Decode
LLM + heuristic extraction of narrative features from title text: conflict density, stakes, warmth, humor, darkness, hook, 3-act valence arcs.
03Signal
Features modeled against fused ground truth on each surface — theatrical economics, ad delivery, streaming demand, social velocity — into the five-signal profile.

The spine is the moat. Anyone can prompt an LLM about a script. Only HIT Predictor can test the answer against what audiences — and markets — actually did.

Where it stands — and why we publish it

The v0.1 pilot found real signal in summary text — and proved it isn't enough.

Nearly 10,000 titles scored across two runs on the linear-audience surface. Marketing-copy proxies produce replicable but small effects (<2% of variance) — a finding most vendors would bury. We publish it, because it defines the build: the levers are script-level text, per-demographic targets, and time-series windows — all on the spine already. Candor is the credibility. The same discipline — title-integrity guards, certified crosswalks, dedup audits — is what makes every signal trustable when it lands.

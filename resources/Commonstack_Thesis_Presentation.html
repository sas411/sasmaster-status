01 · The Problem

Two failures. One root cause.

FAILURE 1Rationed and blind

Flagship programs are drowning, not idle. CA's 9% housing credit ran 0.00 applications per award in 2024, 0.00 in 2025. The $20B GGRF freeze sat frozen 17+ months — grantees winning in court, still unable to touch the money.

FAILURE 2The entry margin is closed

Navigation cost is fixed per pursuit — regressive by size. Each additional funding source on a CA housing deal adds $0 per unit and 0.00 months, on deals stacking 8–12 sources (Terner Center, 2025).

No participant, on either side, holds a view of the whole. Agencies can't see cross-program demand. Applicants can't see the full stack. The system has no shared instrumentation at all.

02 · The Mechanism

A market with no price signal — clearing administratively.

● trades below the floor vanish before they clear. Nobody needs to behave badly for this to occur — it falls straight out of the cost structure.

03 · Why Now

AI collapses the one input that was historically irreducible.

SUPPLY SIDEThe cost floor drops

Rules lived in unstructured prose; interpretation lived in expensive specialists whose cost never fell with scale. When the binding cost of a market's clearing mechanism collapses, its clearing capacity expands.

DEMAND SIDEPolicy velocity rewards position

PPP spawned an overnight navigation industry. ERC built an entire advisory sector. CHIPS and the IRA created the wave Crux monetized. Whoever has infrastructure in place before the window opens captures the surge.

This platform is long AI progress twice: better models lower the cost of producing navigation, while AI-driven disruption expands the administered flows that need navigating.

04 · The Idea

Not a better grant-search tool.
The coordination layer over capital flows.

A cost collapse available to everyone isn't a business — everyone will hold the cheap technology. History says the value accrues to whoever builds the coordination layer on top: electronic trading, internet commerce, and — closer to home — Crux and OpenGov.

INTELLIGENCE → grows at grant-search pace COORDINATION → grows at venture pace

Stitch it into one deliverable.

Pick the findings and catalogue reports you want to put in front of someone. Compose picks up each one's real n, its measured universe, and every limit served with it — then merges those limits into the deliverable instead of quietly dropping them. Denominators never blend. If two sources stand on different universes, the deliverable says so on its front page.

Findings—

Adversarially reconciled analyses. Each carries a served headline figure, a measured universe, and the limits that bound it.

Catalogue reports—

Registered report definitions. These carry structure — the question, the parameters, the chart language — and no measured instance, so they contribute framing to a deliverable and never a figure.

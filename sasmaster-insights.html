Six things the market
is not pricing.

Every claim below survived an adversarial pass: three independent analyses, then a reconciliation that killed more findings than it kept. What is left is small, measured, and honestly bounded — and it disagrees with how content is currently valued, ordered, and sold.

What this page refuses to tell you

  • Any time-shift lift figure. Two independent methods found the stream ladder does not reconcile — the "lift" is negative on a third of telecasts. No lift table ships.
  • Any commercial-minute claim. Those columns are byte-identical copies of the program average. The names are there; the signal is not.
  • Any audience-age claim. Two measurement surfaces carry demo coverage running in opposite directions, and nothing yet says which is authoritative.
  • Any absolute viewer count. The unit is uncertified and the two surfaces disagree by roughly two orders of magnitude. Ratios, medians and ranks only.
  • Any goodness-of-fit statistic. At these sample sizes it would flatter the finding, not test it.
  • Any claim that better-reviewed content underperforms. The audience-weighted version of that pattern is a format-composition effect, and the typical title shows no effect at all. The signal fails; quality is not the variable.

This is the differentiator, not a disclaimer. A finding you can act on is one whose limits you were told before you acted.

How to read all of this.

Three measured universes sit behind these six chapters, and they must never be mixed in one sentence. A measured-title universe of a few hundred titles across three broadcast weeks. A household and person panel across twelve complete weeks. An open catalogue with no audience measurement attached at all. Each chapter states which one it stands on, and what that universe cannot see.

Where a cell is thin, it is marked thin. Where a window is a rerun season, the chart says so on its face. Where a number would be more persuasive than it is true, it is not drawn. Nothing on this page is estimated, smoothed, or filled in.

The catalogue is the substrate. Each finding hands off to the underlying report, where the query, the window, and the row counts are the product.
